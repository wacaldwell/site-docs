# Terms of Service — counselor

**Last updated:** 11 September 2026

counselor is a private, self-hosted Discord bot operated by an individual for
personal use. It is not offered to the public, has no commercial purpose, and is
not available for others to install or use.

By using it, you agree to the following.

## 1. It is not therapy

**counselor is not a therapist, counsellor, psychologist, physician, or licensed
professional of any kind, and it is not a substitute for one.**

It produces text from a language model. It holds no qualification, carries no
duty of care, and has no professional accountability. Nothing it says is
therapy, medical advice, mental-health treatment, diagnosis, or legal advice.

It is a reflective prompt in a conversation — closer to a well-read friend who
occasionally says something useful than to any form of clinical care. Decisions
about your relationship, your health, or your safety should not rest on it.

## 2. It is not an emergency service

**It cannot help in a crisis and must not be relied on in one.** It does not
monitor for emergencies, cannot summon help, and may be offline, paused, rate-
limited, or silent at any moment.

If you or someone else is in danger, contact emergency services. In the United
States, call or text **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or **1-800-799-7233**
(National Domestic Violence Hotline). Outside the US, use your local emergency
number.

## 3. Safety limits, stated plainly

counselor stands down entirely — no counselling, no mediation — when it detects
markers of intimate partner violence, coercive control, or self-harm, and
surfaces crisis resources instead. It stays down until deliberately resumed.

You must understand the limits of that:

- **Its detection is weak and must not be trusted as a screen.** It catches only
  explicit disclosure. Research finds most people do not raise these issues
  unprompted, so it will miss the large majority of cases. Silence from it is
  not clearance, and its willingness to engage is not a finding that a
  relationship is safe.
- **It deliberately does not ask.** Screening in a channel both partners can
  read is not screening, and the question itself can be a risk.
- **It will not help plan around safety** — no exit planning, no advice on
  documenting or on tracking software. Those belong with a trained advocate.

Couples work is contraindicated where there is violence or coercive control.
If that describes your situation, this tool is the wrong tool, and individual
specialised support is the right one.

## 4. It takes no side

It is designed never to adjudicate, never to assign blame, and never to take one
partner's side. It will not tell you who is right. If you are looking for a
ruling, it is the wrong tool.

## 5. It may say nothing, or something wrong

It speaks rarely and by design — cooldowns, daily caps and suppression rules are
enforced in code. Silence is normal operation, not a fault.

Its output is generated by a language model and may be **inaccurate,
inappropriate, poorly timed, or simply wrong**. Treat everything it says as a
suggestion to consider, never as instruction to follow. The operator makes no
representation that it will be helpful, correct, or well judged.

## 6. Consent of everyone in the channel

The bot reads every message in its channel, and its content is sent to a
third-party model provider (see the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html)).
Everyone in that channel must know it is there and agree to it. Do not add it to
a channel where any participant has not consented, and do not use it to capture
or analyse another person's words without their knowledge.

## 8. Ending it

Either party may stop it at any time: remove it from the channel, run
`/counselor forget`, or shut down the host. The operator may modify or
